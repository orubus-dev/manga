# Pre-Destined Character Set — Chapters 1–10

Every character that appears in chapters 1–10, with a locked visual prompt block.
Blocks are injected verbatim into page prompts. **The operative source of truth is
`pipeline/locks.py`** — the quotes below are mirrors. Changes require a change-log
entry in `bible/CHANGELOG.md`.

## GOLDEN BODY-PLAN RULE (learned ch07/08 QC)
All Trilobite-folk locks now state: *strictly human body plan — one head, upright
on two legs, two arms — natural chitin plate armor (armadillo-like), dome shell
head helmet, amber eye lights, no human face, no antennae, not an insect.*
Without this, the model renders literal pillbugs and the acting (kneeling,
weeping, ruling) becomes unreadable.

## YIN SHEN — protagonist (human form, ch1 only)
Appears: ch1 (modern), remembered ch2.
Locked block `@yinshen@`:
> Yin Shen, a young East Asian man with messy black hair and sharp dark eyes, fair
> skin, wearing a dark navy modern hooded jacket with a small white zipper line,
> dark slim pants, black shoes

Notes: calm, detached personality; curious; the scene of his death is a traffic
accident — he clutches the Redlichia fossil box as glass shatters.

## YIN SHEN AS GOD — "Yinsai" (ch2–10, Cambrian era)
Locked block `@god@` (see locks.py — hardened after face-drift QC):
> a tall smooth silhouette of pure glowing white light shaped like a genderless
> human … the head is a smooth blank oval of light with absolutely no facial
> features — no eyes, no nose, no mouth …

Canon: worshippers can never see his face ("unable to make out the features").
Never draw eyes/mouth. He is the only human in the world; bare presence = awe.

## REDLICHIA — Trilobite Man, firstborn, King of Wisdom (ch2–10)
Locked block `@redlichia@`:
> Redlichia the Trilobite Man, a humanoid male covered in glossy black chitin
> carapace plates, his head encased in a three-lobed pale bone helmet with a
> prominent central ridge and deep eye shadows, glowing amber eyes, powerful but
> slender build

Progression (story-driven only): ch2 reverent crawler → ch3-4 worshipper with
offerings → ch5-6 king with people → ch7-9 crowned leader (may add a simple stone
bead necklace once city exists) → ch10 lawgiver. Expressive body language: he feels
joy, sorrow, shame, love.

## SHELLY — Fusion Monster, "Mother of Life" (ch2–10)
Human form locked block `@shelly@`:
> Shelly, a petite little girl with large round green eyes and brown hair in a loose
> braid, barefoot with pink toenails, wearing a flowing translucent white gauzy
> cloak that grows directly from her body like a jellyfish bell

True form locked block `@fmonster@`:
> the Fusion Monster, a colossal mountain of pale flesh shaped like a giant worm,
> bearing an immense spiral ammonite-like shell on its back, sharp spines
> protruding from its body, eerie green eyeballs of many sizes dotting its flesh,
> and a myriad of thick tentacles

Canon: her human form is a shape she took to please the god; her gaze terrifies the
Trilobite Men to their bloodline. She speaks only in "Gurgle, gurgle!"

## THE WHITE QUEEN — first female Trilobite Man (ch6, ch10)
Design note: shares Redlichia's locked trilobite-folk body plan (dome head shield,
overlapping chiton segments, claw hands), rendered in pearl-white with a softer,
narrower frame. Standalone portrait attempts drifted humanoid 3x — body-plan sharing
is the locked solution. Always describe her via Redlichia's plan + white coloring.
Locked block `@queen@`:
> the queen, a female trilobite person with the same smooth semicircular dome head
> shield and overlapping chitin segments as Redlichia but in pearl-white with a soft
> nacre sheen, a slender graceful narrower frame, two gentle amber eye lights glowing
> under the front rim of her dome, no human face
