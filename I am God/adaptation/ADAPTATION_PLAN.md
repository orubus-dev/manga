## Chapter 09 — Food and a New Species
*Palette: city · Source: novel/ch009.txt*

### Page 49 (sheet 1)
- **Art:** - @sons@ standing high on the palace platform, one holding an @afish@ aloft for all to see, proclaiming to the square below.      - the square filled with @tmen@ gazing up, sunlight refracting through the raised fish.      - the second son with arms raised high, voice thunderous, fervor bordering on madness.
- **Dialogue:** “Behold! This is the food bestowed upon us by God's grace!”
- **Dialogue:** “Starting today, no one in the God-Given City will starve to death — and we will no longer need to risk the deep sea.”
- **Dialogue:** “Yinsai God!”

### Page 50 (sheet 1)
- **Art:** - @redlichia@ calm and regal beside his excited sons, composed where they are feverish.      - he raises one hand and the crowd roars, voices overturning the entire city.      - the marshes — trilobite people carrying large stone basins of Ancestral Fish, pouring them into the swamps, water splashing, everyone laughing.
- **Dialogue:** “We are a species beloved by God! We are destined to become the most glorious civilization, forever shining beneath the stars.”
- **Dialogue:** “Hurry, hurry! Over there too!”

### Page 51 (sheet 1)
- **Art:** - stones blocking the tidal connections, enclosing the marsh like a vast stone-bound pond.      - trilobite people kneeling in a welcoming row as @redlichia@ approaches the water.      - his gaze settling on his eldest daughter — @daughter@ standing among the hopeful sons.
- **Dialogue:** “From this day forth, you are responsible for guarding and protecting the Ancestral Fish — our food, and the future of this land.”
- **Narration:** They blocked the tidal connections with stones, enclosing the area.

### Page 52 (sheet 2)
- **Art:** - @daughter@ kneeling with surprise and joy.      - female trilobite folk gathering around her at the marshes.      - they dive into the water, herding the fish shoals beneath the surface like shepherds tending their flocks.
- **Dialogue:** “Great King of Wisdom! I will surely fulfill the duties you have entrusted to me.”
- **Narration:** The female Trilobite Men farmed the Ancestral Fish in the marshes, like shepherds tending their flocks.

### Page 53 (sheet 2)
- **Art:** - the harvest — Ancestral Fish caught by the hundred, the shore a scene of jubilation.      - @daughter@ standing by the stone-enclosed swamp, watching the fish radiate vitality.      - the crowd cheering and leaping for the bountiful harvest.
- **Dialogue:** “Life is truly full of miracles! God can easily create and bestow such miracles — what an incredible power.”
- **Narration:** With this, they had finally overcome the troubles of food.

### Page 54 (sheet 2)
- **Art:** - a quiet omen — inside the marsh, several @afish@ darting suddenly toward the depths.      - they discover a narrow passage leading to the open ocean, dark water beyond.      - the lead fish squeezing into the opening, bubbles rising, the vast black sea swallowing the scene.
- **Narration:** The Ancestral Fish left the marshes, venturing into the vast sea.
- **Narration:** From this moment, a new species emerged in the ocean.

## Chapter 10 — Writing and Mythology
*Palette: city · Source: novel/ch010.txt*

### Page 55 (sheet 1)
- **Art:** - @redlichia@ on the high walls of the royal palace, surveying the flourishing God-Given City — salt-drying yards, workshops, warehouses, massive wells connecting to the sea.      - the marsh farmland beyond — female trilobite folk leaping into the water, herding and catching fish shoals.      - the city bustling in warm golden light.
- **Narration:** With the food problem resolved, the God-Given City thoroughly stabilized and began to flourish and develop.

### Page 56 (sheet 1)
- **Art:** - @redlichia@ and @queen@ standing together on the high walls, the setting sun before them.      - the queen's white dome tilted toward him with quiet adoration.      - claw and claw clasped together, the sea burning gold behind them.
- **Dialogue:** “I once made a vow to God to create a great civilization that would never fade. For ten thousand years, a hundred thousand years, a million years. Until… Eternity.”
- **Dialogue:** “You are the King of Wisdom, the firstborn of God. You will surely accomplish it.”

### Page 57 (sheet 1)
- **Art:** - a towering white stone stele at the city entrance, carved front and back — God descending upon land and sea, guiding the people to this blessed place.      - @redlichia@ studying the carvings, something nagging at him.      - his claw touching the blank space at the stele's edge.
- **Dialogue:** “That's right. There's no writing yet.”
- **Narration:** Rather than an epic mythology, it seemed like savages carving miraculous chapters on cave walls.

### Page 58 (sheet 2)
- **Art:** - inside the temple, @god@ walking slowly along the wall beside the transparent shell-tank, watching the fish inside.      - @redlichia@ following behind him, asking earnestly.      - the god turning, his glow steady and calm.
- **Dialogue:** “God! You haven't taught Redlichia the divine script!”
- **Dialogue:** “My writing belongs to me. You should create a writing system unique to you.”

### Page 59 (sheet 2)
- **Art:** - the god's finger drawing a glowing circle of light on the shell surface, a beam of light trailing.      - more symbols appearing — a crescent, wavy lines — and @redlichia@ drawing a wavy line with his own claw, excited.      - the shell's surface filling with strange glowing symbols, Redlichia's eyes shining, his claw-hands clenched.
- **Dialogue:** “This is the sun!”
- **Dialogue:** “This is the moon!”
- **Dialogue:** “This is water… This is the sea!”
- **Narration:** Under God's guidance, one initial character after another was created.

### Page 60 (sheet 2)
- **Art:** - the stele at the city entrance now covered with pictograms and carved characters — the sun symbol, wave lines, the myth of God and the King of Wisdom.      - trilobite people gathered before it, gazing up at the symbols, clever ones pointing and guessing the meanings.      - sunset over the God-Given City — the pyramid, the city, the marsh farms, the sea — a civilization born.
- **Dialogue:** “So this is what God is like.”
- **Narration:** With the emergence of writing, the civilization of the Trilobite Men began to develop further — truly deserving the title of a civilization.
